# SportNear visual plan

## Reference mapping

Home follows the supplied tennis-court concept: photographic opening, large headline, stacked Training and Play & Clubs cards, frosted captions and circular green arrows.

Sport selection follows the supplied six-sport concept: bold heading, two-column photographic tiles, glass labels and manual emirate selection.

Results follow the supplied football concept: sport photograph behind the title, compact filters, stacked photo-and-information glass cards, clear AED session pricing and green detail arrows.

## Design system

Deep green-grey backdrop, pale grey translucent panels, fine white edges and emerald accents. Glass uses actual backdrop blur, with solid fallbacks where blur is unavailable or reduced transparency is requested. Native system typography uses a strong heading hierarchy and quiet secondary text.

## Portrait delivery

The app uses a maximum 460px vertical frame on large screens and an edge-to-edge layout on phones. Bottom navigation is fixed, with reserved content space beneath it. The generated photographs recreate the mood of the references; they are not identical to the supplied images.

## Functional scope

Retain both discovery flows, all sports and emirates, filters, price sorting, details and fictional sample notices. Implement Saved as a local shortlist. No fake iOS status bar, bookings or real-provider claims.
